The product

ContentSpark is a free browser-based social caption, hook, and hashtag generator for social media creators. You type a topic. The free tool builds captions, thread ideas, hashtags, and video hooks from templates that run in your browser. The free tool does not require an account.

ContentSpark Pro is an optional digital subscription at $9 per month. Pro is unlocked in the browser when you enter a license key from Lemon Squeezy. While Pro is active, the topic you type is sent to a ContentSpark server so an AI model can write the content. If that request fails, the page uses the free template generator instead. The browser also stops Pro AI at 30 generations per day and then uses the free templates until the next day.

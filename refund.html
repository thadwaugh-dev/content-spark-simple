Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated November 5, 2026. This page describes how ContentSpark works in plain language. It is not legal advice.

What you are paying for

ContentSpark Pro is a digital subscription at $9 per month. It unlocks AI generation in the browser with a Lemon Squeezy license key. The free generator is $0. There is no charge to refund on the free tool.

Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy takes payment, calculates tax, and emails the receipt. Thad Waugh, who operates ContentSpark, provides the software.

Cancel anytime

You can cancel ContentSpark Pro anytime. Open the receipt email from Lemon Squeezy and use the customer portal link in that email. Cancellation stops future renewal charges. Lemon Squeezy applies the cancellation to the subscription.

Refunds within 14 days

You can request a refund within 14 days of a charge. Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} from, or with, the email address on your Lemon Squeezy receipt, and say you want that charge refunded. Thad Waugh will refund the charge through Lemon Squeezy. A refunded license can stop unlocking Pro.

Charges older than 14 days are not refunded under this policy. You can still cancel so the subscription does not renew.
